# ✈️ TravelMate AI — Multi-Agent Travel Planner with LangGraph

> An AI-powered travel planning system that transforms natural-language travel requests into personalized, weather-aware, budget-conscious trip plans using multiple specialized AI agents.

TravelMate AI is an end-to-end **Multi-Agent AI Travel Planning application** built using:

**LangGraph · LangChain · Groq · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · Tavily · AviationStack · MCP**

Instead of manually searching across multiple platforms for flights, hotels, weather, budgets, and sightseeing, TravelMate AI coordinates specialized AI agents through a LangGraph workflow and generates a complete travel plan from a single natural-language request.

---

# 🚀 Overview

Planning a trip usually requires searching across multiple platforms for:

- ✈️ Flights
- 🏨 Hotels
- 🌤️ Weather and forecast
- 📍 Places to visit
- 🗓️ Day-by-day itinerary
- 💰 Budget estimation
- 🚗 Local transportation
- 🍜 Food recommendations
- 👤 Human review and approval

TravelMate AI combines these tasks into a single intelligent workflow.

Users can simply describe their travel requirements naturally.

### Example

```text
Plan a 7-day trip to Nepal from India for 2 people
under ₹2 lakhs including flights, hotels, sightseeing,
food, weather information and local transport.
